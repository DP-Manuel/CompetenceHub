# Priority A Content Inventory

Status: baseline completed from repository content on 2026-09-04; no public
copy was changed by this inventory.

## Scope And Method

This inventory covers `/`, `/unternehmen`, `/leistungen`,
`/businesscoaching` and `/mindforge`. It records what the current pages say,
which audience and use case they serve, which first-party information is
already available, and where editorial or evidence work remains.

Only versioned project files and current website source were used. Private raw
sources, `Quellen/`, `.env`, `.tmp/`, credentials and personal or customer data
were not opened or copied. Existing publication is not treated automatically
as proof of a claim.

Classical SEO remains the foundation. GEO adds clear answers, attributable
expertise and traceable first-party evidence; it does not replace titles,
descriptions, semantic HTML, internal links, performance or accessibility.

## Cross-Page Findings

1. The current journey is coherent: concern, classification, expertise, format
   and contact recur across all five routes.
2. `/unternehmen` currently has the strongest approved first-party authority
   signal through the Concept Clean customer voice. The illustrative story
   explains process but must never be counted as customer evidence.
3. The other four routes depend mainly on first-party descriptions of the
   offer. They still need attributable expertise or approved practice evidence
   before stronger authority claims are made.
4. Audience overlap is greatest on `/leistungen` and `/mindforge`.
   `/businesscoaching` also mixes buyer and participant perspectives.
5. Editorial ownership, subject-matter review dates and commercial review are
   not yet consistently recorded. Those are governance gaps, not copy gaps.
6. No page needs additional word count merely to satisfy a target. The GEO
   video's percentages and 500-2,000-word range remain research orientation
   only and are not acceptance criteria.

## Inventory Exit Criteria

- All five priority routes record audience, use case, question, expertise,
  primary information, CTA, gaps and owner/review state.
- Mixed audiences and overlapping service boundaries are visible.
- Existing content is not promoted to evidence without a documented source or
  approval.
- No new guide page, quotation, case, statistic or customer reference was
  created.
