## Status Vocabulary

- **Approved:** evidence and publication boundary are documented for the exact
  current use.
- **Available - review needed:** first-party information exists, but factual,
  commercial, rights or subject-matter approval is incomplete or not recorded.
- **Missing:** no suitable evidence is inventoried. The gap must remain empty
  until real material is supplied and approved.
- **Not required:** the field does not currently add useful support to the page
  purpose.

Current public copy is not automatically an approved evidence item. Private raw
sources, credentials, personal data and customer data are excluded from this
matrix.

## Research Orientation Boundary

The GEO-video figures of roughly 30-40% visibility improvement and roughly
500-2,000 words are recorded only as research orientation. They are not a
promise, KPI, acceptance criterion or fixed page-length rule. Page quality is
judged by intent fit, factual support, source clarity, usefulness, technical
SEO, accessibility and approved next steps.
