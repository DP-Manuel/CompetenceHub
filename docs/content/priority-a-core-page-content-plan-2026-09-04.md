# Priority A Core Page Content Plan

Status: planning draft completed on 2026-09-04; stakeholder decisions and
content approvals are open. This document authorizes no public copy change.

## Purpose

The five Priority A routes should answer different questions while preserving
one coherent journey:

`Anliegen -> Klärung -> passende Expertise -> passendes Format -> nächster Schritt`

The plan uses the completed Content Inventory and Content-Evidence Matrix. It
does not create guide pages, expert quotations, customer claims, statistics or
new commercial promises.

## Route Architecture

| Route | One primary job | Boundary to the other routes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Explain the Hub and route an unresolved concern | Does not compare every service in detail or act as a full company/product page |
| `/unternehmen` | Help a company classify a need and understand the matching path | Owns company-buyer context and approved company practice evidence, not every coaching method |
| `/leistungen` | Compare service and format categories | Acts as the taxonomy and routing overview, not as a second homepage or a collection of thin service claims |
| `/businesscoaching` | Explain when Businesscoaching fits a work-related situation | Owns method fit and coaching-versus-consulting boundary; does not duplicate the full company journey or Mindforge brand story |
| `/mindforge` | Explain the branded Mindforge offer, process, audience paths and limits | Owns Mindforge-specific positioning; company sections link back to company context rather than replacing `/unternehmen` |

## Standard Expert-Input Template

Use this template only to collect real input. Do not prefill answers or publish
the result without factual, quotation-rights and editorial approval.

1. Expert name and approved public role.
2. Route and concrete user question addressed.
3. Situation in which the expertise is useful.
4. Situation in which another approach is more suitable.
5. Short expert answer in the expert's own reviewed words.
6. Practice observation without customer identity or unsupported outcome.
7. Qualification or experience basis that may be stated publicly.
8. Supporting primary source, if an external factual claim is made.
9. Approval scope, approver and date.
10. Review or expiry date.

## Planned Verification For A Later Rewrite

- Exact source-to-copy comparison for every evidence-backed statement.
- Unique primary intent, title, description and H1 per route.
- Internal-link test with no circular dead ends or competing primary CTAs.
- Desktop and exact 390-pixel visual QA with zero horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard, focus, landmarks, headings and reduced-motion accessibility checks.
- Astro check/build, link smoke and crawler-policy verification.
- Named factual, commercial, rights and stakeholder approvals recorded before
  production deployment.

## Exit Criteria

- Every Priority A route has one primary job, answer direction, evidence plan,
  limitation, internal-link path, CTA and explicit decision owner type.
- Cross-route overlap is addressed through boundaries rather than duplicated
  copy.
- Stakeholder decisions are visible and can be requested early.
- No public implementation or publication occurred.
