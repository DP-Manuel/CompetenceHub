flows. Rich target_version1 opts into object evidence; old version0 rows keep
their historical semantics. New revisions never inherit evidence. Qualification
and family identities are (Coach UUID, stable key), independent of display title.

public output. Complete old content cannot silently overwrite a richer revision.

## Findings Closed And Residual Limits

1. Old snapshot serialization could erase rich fields. Domain protection now
   recursively checks nested models/keyed collections; import uses supplied-field
   presence before normalization. Omission conflicts; explicit NULL/empty clears
   remain allowed. Focused and real-PG regressions cover both paths.
2. Adding a family with nested old variants selected a descendant disclosure for
   focus. The new direct-child disclosure now opens and focuses its first editable
   field; stable key stays readonly. Native test exercises the actual interaction.
3. Long literal family titles/legends overflowed390px. Scoped wrapping fixes the
   Coach area without changing other Portal screens. Native glyph/range checks
   and actual screenshots now pass. No hiding overflow to make the test green.

Review found no remaining confirmed High/Critical issue in this scope; the two
Medium omission findings are repaired and regression-covered. This is not a
production penetration test or assistive-technology certification. No NVDA/JAWS,
