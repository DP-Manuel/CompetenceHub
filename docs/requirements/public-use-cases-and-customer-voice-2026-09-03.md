between need clarification, expertise, format, delivery and feedback without

## Evidence Boundary

outcome. The public version therefore excludes the draft's unsupported or
unapproved details, including:

- a 48-hour response commitment;
- fixed package names, session counts or commercial terms;
- click-based legal acceptance;
- a claimed 5/5 rating;
- guaranteed workload, conflict or team-climate improvements.

The Concept Clean story may state only what the supplied feedback supports:

- a communication course for employees was booked through Donner + Partner;
- the path from request to delivery was described as smooth;
- flexible matching was valued;
- the course focused on sector-specific terminology and daily team
  communication;
- scheduling fitted the employees' working hours.

No additional outcome, participant count, date, price or named individual may
be inferred.
